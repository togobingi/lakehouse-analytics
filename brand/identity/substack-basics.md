Each issue mixes three things: what shipped and why it matters for your work, a perspective on where BI and AI on the lakehouse are heading, and a real problem we keep seeing in the field with a practical way to solve it.

We are Robin and Tobi, two architects who spend our days helping teams get dashboards, semantic layers and AI assistants into production. Everything we write is based on public sources, and the opinions are our own.
