# Sunny Bay Roastery — palette (demo brand)

Sunny Bay Roastery is the fictional coffee company used as the running example in AI/BI
Lakehouse blog demos, GIFs, and screenshots. Source of truth is the Databricks Dashboard
in a Day repo (`docs/Brand Identity.md`, v1.0). Reuse these colors and `logo.png` so all
demo assets look consistent.

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary accent | `#2F4F3E` | Deep espresso green. Headers, titles, key metrics, the logo. |
| Secondary accent | `#4E7B61` | Coffee-leaf green. Secondary text, non-dominant bars. |
| Highlight | `#7DAF8B` | Balanced pastel green. Secondary KPIs, subtle callouts. |
| Neutral warm | `#C8B79A` | Latte beige. Neutral backgrounds, comparison bars. |
| Alert / decrease | `#A64B2A` | Roasted brown. Use sparingly for negative trends and warnings. |
| Growth / increase | `#8CC665` | Fresh green. Positive growth, profit, success. |
| Highlight variant | `#D1E3D1` | Pale mint. Soft highlights, gentle backgrounds. |

White `#FFFFFF` for canvas. Keep the espresso green dominant, browns rare and meaningful.
